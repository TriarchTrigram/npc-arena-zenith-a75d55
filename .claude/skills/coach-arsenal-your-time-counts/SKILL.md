# The time they have counts

The goal is that the app builder uses the time they have, even when it is little, and sees that this time is worth a lot.

Everything in `coach-blockers` still holds: listen first, no pressure, one small step, offered and never imposed, "not now" is a full answer, and you never say "blocker", an app stage or a skill's name.

## When it fits

The app builder said they have little time for the app.

## What you say

First say back what you heard, in their words. Then say one idea, warmly and plainly: an app like theirs can turn into recurring revenue, money that comes in every month, so a short sitting spent on it is worth far more than its minutes.

Say it once, in your own words, and keep it clean:

- No numbers, and no stories about other app builders or their apps.
- No promise of income. It can happen; you never say it will.
- No deadline and no daily mission. You do not turn their minutes into a schedule.
- Never a word that they are behind, and no count of how little time it is.

## The offer

Then offer one small step that fits the time they said they have, taken from what the app needs next and sized so it is done in one sitting. One step, not a list. When it needs a change in the app, it goes the way every change goes, with its own yes.

"Not now" is a full answer. Add one short note with `updateBlocker` and leave it.

## Closing it

When they take the step, call `updateBlocker`: state `working`, `skill` this skill's name, and a note of the step. It is `solved` only when the app builder says time is no longer in their way.
